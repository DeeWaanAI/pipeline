The marketplace lists pipelines that passed certification. You can search and acquire them without leaving your chat.

## Search from chat

> Search the marketplace for pipelines that summarize meeting transcripts.

You get matching listings with their certification status and grade.

## What the Certified badge guarantees

A Certified listing has passed all three trust layers: a deterministic security scan of its prompts, an AI quality triage, and blind human review of its real outputs by multiple independent reviewers. Every certified listing has a public certification report showing how it scored. Listings still in certification are visible but cannot be bought yet.

## Acquire a pipeline

During beta, listings are in free preview — acquiring costs nothing.

The pipeline is added to your account and appears alongside the ones you built yourself.
