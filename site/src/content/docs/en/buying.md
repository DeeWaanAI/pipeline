The marketplace lists pipelines that passed certification. Listings are shared free of charge, as open source — nothing is sold. You can search and fork them without leaving your chat.

## Search from chat

> Search the marketplace for pipelines that summarize meeting transcripts.

You get matching listings with their certification status and grade.

## What the Certified badge guarantees

A listing you can fork has been published with its evidence: a completed test recorded by the platform, scored by three judges from three different model developers with none of them a model that generated the answers, a scan of the disclosed material for secrets, and the author's own consent to the exact preview that went public. Read the record on the listing's evidence page — the inputs, the outputs, the full judging prompts, every score, and every failure. The checks are structural: they prove the evidence is complete and honestly sourced, not that anyone rated it excellent, and a listing whose evidence falls short simply never gets published.

## Fork a pipeline

Forking is free — sharing is the whole point of the marketplace.

> Fork the "meeting summarizer" pipeline.

A copy is created in your account — as a project you own — and appears alongside the ones you built yourself. The copy stays attributed to its original author, and the signed evidence record travels with it, bound to the exact version you received.

## Run a forked pipeline

Run it exactly like your own pipelines:

> Run the meeting summarizer with this transcript: …

Runs use your own provider keys. A forked pipeline is a full copy — including its prompts — so once you edit it, your version no longer matches the evidence record, and the record says so rather than pretending otherwise.
